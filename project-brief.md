##The Question?
How has excessive rainfall impacted Roads in Port Harcourt within the span of 2025-2026?

##Why it Matters
-To show whether road vulnerability to excessive rainfall has increased, decreased, or remained similar between 2025 and 2026.

-To Identify which road are most vulnerable and show where action or maintenance should be prioritised across Port Harcourt.

-To aid future urban planning and constructions.

##The Build
An interactive GIS dashboard that maps rainfall-affected and high-risk roads in Port Harcourt, allowing an officer to compare road vulnerability between 2025 and 2026 and identify areas requiring attention.
